## Deletion Requests（删除请求）

若有参与者在会后要求删除数据，请在此记录：

```markdown
### Deletion log
- 2025-10-xx: Session ID 7a9f2c — All rows removed from data/metrics.csv and corresponding notes anonymised, at participant request.
```
