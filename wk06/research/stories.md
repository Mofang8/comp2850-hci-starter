# Job Stories — Week 6 Needs-Finding

## Story S1: Filter Persistence
**Situation**: When I'm filtering tasks by a category (e.g., project tag) and perform an action (like adding a task)
**Motivation**: I want the filter selection to persist across page reloads
**Outcome**: So I can continue working in the same context without re-selecting the filter
**Underlying need**: Because re-filtering repeatedly creates cognitive overload and breaks flow

**Evidence**: Participant A (notes Q1)
**Inclusion risk**: Cognitive, Motor (extra clicks), Memory
**Type**: Job story
**Priority**: Medium

---

## Story S2: Explicit Confirmation
**Situation**: When I submit a form (add or delete a task)
**Motivation**: I want clear, immediate visual and auditory confirmation that the action succeeded
**Outcome**: So I can trust the system and avoid duplicate submissions
**Underlying need**: Because uncertainty leads to anxiety and errors (duplicates)

**Evidence**: Participant A (notes Q2), Participant B (notes Q2)
**Inclusion risk**: Cognitive, Low digital literacy, Screen Reader
**Type**: Job story
**Priority**: High (WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages)

---

## Story S3: Efficient Keyboard Navigation
**Situation**: When I am navigating a long list of tasks using only the keyboard
**Motivation**: I want to skip repetitive controls or navigate efficiently to specific actions
**Outcome**: So I don't have to press Tab dozens of times to delete a specific item
**Underlying need**: Because excessive keystrokes cause physical pain (RSI) and fatigue

**Evidence**: Participant A (notes Q3)
**Inclusion risk**: Motor impairment, RSI, Keyboard users
**Type**: Job story
**Priority**: High (WCAG 2.1.1 Keyboard, 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks)

---

## Story S4: Sufficient Contrast
**Situation**: When I am using the app in bright sunlight or with low vision
**Motivation**: I want text and interactive elements to have high contrast against the background
**Outcome**: So I can read the content and identify buttons without straining my eyes
**Underlying need**: Because low contrast makes the interface unusable in certain environmental conditions

**Evidence**: Participant B (notes Q1)
**Inclusion risk**: Low vision, Situational (sunlight), Elderly
**Type**: Job story
**Priority**: High (WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast)

---

## Story S5: Assertive Error Feedback
**Situation**: When I submit invalid data (e.g., empty task title)
**Motivation**: I want the error to be announced immediately and clearly
**Outcome**: So I know exactly what went wrong and how to fix it without searching the page
**Underlying need**: Because silent failures leave screen reader users confused and stuck

**Evidence**: Participant B (notes Q2)
**Inclusion risk**: Screen Reader, Cognitive
**Type**: Job story
**Priority**: High (WCAG 3.3.1 Error Identification, 4.1.3 Status Messages)

---

## Story S6: Task List Management (Pagination/Filter)
**Situation**: When I have a large number of tasks (e.g., 50+)
**Motivation**: I want to view them in manageable chunks or filter by category
**Outcome**: So I don't have to scroll through a "mile-long" page
**Underlying need**: Because information overload makes it hard to focus and find specific items

**Evidence**: Participant B (notes Q3)
**Inclusion risk**: Cognitive (Attention), Motor (excessive scrolling)
**Type**: Job story
**Priority**: Medium (Planned for Week 8)
